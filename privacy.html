Privacy Policy

Castix processes signed Telegram authentication data to verify identity. The service may store Telegram user ID, username, language, registration date, favorites, submission identifiers and dates, theme metadata, review status and reasons, files and optional artwork. Approved theme names, creator names, descriptions and artwork may be published in the community and channel. Download totals are recorded. Google Apps Script, Sheets and Drive provide processing and storage; Vercel hosts bot and download endpoints; Telegram provides authentication, messages and file delivery; GitHub Pages hosts this website. These providers may process technical request data under their own policies. Language preference is stored locally. AdsGram may provide sponsored tasks inside Telegram and process Telegram identifiers and technical interaction data under its own policies. Public browser pages do not request ads. Data is retained as needed for operation and moderation; no fixed retention period is established. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion. Public copies and provider backups may remain outside our control.
