Content Guidelines

Submit original or authorized .cas themes up to 2 MB with accurate names, versions, categories and descriptions. Optional icons and previews must represent the theme accurately. Do not submit malicious files, unlawful content, impersonation or copyrighted material without permission. All submissions are subject to moderation.
